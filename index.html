Rooster Cam support

Help with connecting to cameras and Color-Con lighting devices.

Connection help

Connect your iPhone or iPad to the same local network as the device you want to control. Check that the device is powered on and its network address is correct. Allow Local Network access for Rooster Cam in iOS Settings.

Saved colors

Saved colors are stored by the app on your device. Keep a backup of important settings before deleting an app or changing devices.

Privacy policy

Effective November 5, 2026

This policy describes Rooster Cam, an app from DBM, LLC.

Information in the app

Rooster Cam stores settings and saved colors on your device. The app communicates over your local network with the cameras and lighting devices you choose to connect, to display information and send control commands. DBM, LLC does not collect this information from the app.

Sharing and Apple services

If you choose to share or export information, it is sent to the destination you select. Apple provides the App Store and may process information in connection with downloads and device services under its own privacy policy.
